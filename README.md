## 🎯 Key Topics Covered
- **Introduction to Economic Growth Policies:** Short-run support vs. long-run productive capacity improvement[
- **Fiscal Policy:** Government spending, taxation, expansionary & contractionary measures, and Aggregate Demand (AD) shifts.
- **Monetary Policy:** Central bank roles (Bangladesh Bank), interest-rate channels, and money supply management.
- **Human Capital Development:** Role of education, technical training, and healthcare in boosting productivity.
- **Investment & Infrastructure Development:** Encouraging private investment, Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), and physical/digital infrastructure.
- **Technology & Innovation:** Digitalization, automation, AI, R&D, and Long-Run Aggregate Supply (LRAS) shifts.
- **The Business Cycle Model:** Recovery, expansion, peak, contraction, and recession vs. depression dynamics.
